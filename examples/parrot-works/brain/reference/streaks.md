title: "Streaks"
tags: [reference, glossary, product]
---
Consecutive days with at least one finished lesson. Rolls over at local midnight.
Streak freezes cover one missed day; lapsed learners get a freeze offer.
Gotcha: [[lessons/utc13-breaks-streak-rollover]]. Rule: [[decisions/streaks-use-user-timezone]].
