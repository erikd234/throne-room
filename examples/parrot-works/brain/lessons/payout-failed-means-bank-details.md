---
title: "Payout failed usually means stale bank details"
tags: [lesson, support, stripe]
learned: 2026-09-20
---
Most Stripe payout.failed events for creators come from closed or renamed bank accounts.
Ask for updated details in the first reply; do not retry the payout blindly.
Used in [[playbooks/handle-payout-failures]]. See [[reference/stripe]].
