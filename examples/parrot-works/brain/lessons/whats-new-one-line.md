---
title: "What's New is one or two lines"
tags: [lesson, marketing, app-store]
learned: 2026-09-15
---
Sectioned "What's New" notes got no more taps than one line and read like a changelog.
Write one or two spunky lines; set them in App Store Connect only after approval.
